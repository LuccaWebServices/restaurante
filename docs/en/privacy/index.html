2. In short

  • This website does not use cookies, analytics tools, advertising or social media pixels.

3. Website hosting and technical data

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. (United States). When you visit the website, GitHub’s servers automatically log technical data such as your IP address, the date and time of the request and your browser type, which are needed to deliver the pages and protect the service against abuse. We do not use this data to identify you.

  • Purpose: to provide the website securely.
  • Legal basis: legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Retention: for the period set by GitHub (see the GitHub Privacy Statement (opens in a new tab)).
  • International transfers: GitHub may process data outside the European Economic Area, with the safeguards provided for in the GDPR (for example, the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses).

4. Preferences stored on your device

The website stores the following in your browser’s local storage (localStorage), on your device only:

  • the language you chose (Portuguese or English);
  • the visual theme chosen in the demo version;
  • your choice to load the map automatically, if you tick “Remember my choice”.

This data is not a cookie, is never sent to any server and cannot identify you. It is strictly necessary for the feature you asked for. You can delete it at any time in your browser settings (site data).

5. Google map

The Google map is only loaded if you click “Load map”. Until then, no data is sent to Google. When it loads, Google Ireland Limited receives your IP address and may set cookies, as an independent controller, under the Google Privacy Policy (opens in a new tab).

Legal basis: your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). If you ticked “Remember my choice”, you can withdraw consent by deleting this website’s data in your browser.

Links to other services

The website links to third-party services (for example, booking and delivery platforms, WhatsApp, social media and map apps). When you click them, you leave this website and that service’s privacy policy applies.
